Projects

Attack–Defense Platform for Cybersecurity Training and Competitions

A flexible Attack–Defense platform tailored for education, training, and competitions.

  • Preconfigured containerized environment containing vulnerable services so participants can both defend and attack.
  • Automated match scheduling, flag rotation, service health-checking, and scoreboard updates.
  • Instructor customization (number/type of vulnerabilities, flag locations via environment variables, bot health scripts).
  • Automatic flag updates and point recalculations to simulate continuous engagement.
Technical notes & features

Implemented using container orchestration (Docker + orchestration scripts), health bots, and a central match scheduler. Services are run in isolated containers with configurable flags exposed to participants. Scoreboard updates are real-time and fault-tolerant with periodic integrity checks.
